# Team Tab — plan (from the whiteboard)

**Status:** planning. The only thing built so far is the create-activity change (#1 below);
the Team Tab itself is proposed here for review.

**Access legend:** **F** = faculty access · **S** = student access.

---

## 1. Create-activity = upload, not wizard  ✅ (built)

Selecting **Generate Activity** (or the "Create a team Activity" option) in the Create tab now
reveals an **inline upload field** (drag & drop / Upload files / Use sample PDF) instead of routing
to the wizard. Adding a source enables **Generate activity**, which opens the drafted activity in
the builder. The wizard remains available for Lessons. *(Done — `src/faculty/CreatePage.tsx`.)*

---

## 2. The Team Tab — a persistent team workspace

Distinct from the **per-activity** team stage (prep → discussion → submission → assessment): the
Team Tab is a **project-team space that persists across activities** — where a team keeps its
resources, records discussions, and accrues an auto-generated progress report.

| Feature | Access | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| **Make Team** | **F** | Faculty creates/edits teams (already exists — Roster & teams / course bar) |
| **View Team** | **F · S** | Team composition, roles, members — read-only for students |
| **Record Audio Discussion** | **S · F** | Students record/upload the team's discussion audio; faculty can play it back |
| **Feedback / Summary** | **S · F** | A shared feedback/summary view → drives the **AI Project Progress Report** |
| **↳ Project Progress Report** | **F · S** | **Auto-generated by AI** from the team's resources + progress + discussions |
| **Team Resources** | **S** | Students add **Proposal · Report · Contract · Whiteboard photos** |
| **↳ "progress is added"** | — | Each resource/update **feeds the AI progress report** |
| **Peer evaluation (CATME-style)** | **F · S** | *maybe* — students rate teammates; faculty sees the adjustment factor |

**Key data-model additions** (new, behind the services layer so §11-style access is enforced there):
`TeamResource { teamId, kind: proposal|report|contract|whiteboard, file, addedBy, addedAt }`,
`AudioDiscussion { teamId, activityId?, audioUrl, recordedBy, at }`,
`ProgressReport { teamId, generatedAt, summary, sources[] }` (AI ✦, regenerated as resources are added).
Access is enforced in a `teamTabService`: students can write their own team's resources/audio;
faculty read everything; the progress report is AI-drafted and faculty-reviewable.

---

## 3. Student experience — **My** tab + **Team** tab  (thought #2)

Restructure the student sidebar into two top-level areas:

- **My** (S) — the individual's own progress: My activities, Individual prep, my submissions,
  Grades & feedback. (This is essentially today's student flow, grouped.)
- **Team** (S) — the Team Tab: View Team, Team Resources (add proposal/report/contract/whiteboard),
  Record Audio Discussion, Feedback/Summary + the Project Progress Report, and peer evaluation.

So a student toggles between "how am *I* doing" (My) and "what is my *team* doing" (Team).

---

## 4. Faculty placement — where the team tab lives  (thought #3, needs planning)

Faculty nav is fixed at **Create · Analytics · AI tutor · Library** (no new top-level item), so the
faculty view of the Team Tab must fold into one of those. Two candidates:

- **Recommended: Analytics → a "Teams" view.** Faculty use the team workspace to *monitor* — read
  progress reports, review resources, play discussion audio, check peer-eval factors. That's
  oversight, which is what Analytics is for. Team *setup* ("Make Team") stays in **Roster & teams**
  (off the course bar). This keeps the four-item nav intact and matches the F-access rows above.
- **Alternative: Course content → a "Teams" subtab** (beside Lessons · Summatives · Activities).
  Cleaner if we think of teams as first-class "content," but it mixes authoring content with team
  monitoring.

**Recommendation:** Analytics for the faculty monitoring view; Roster & teams (course bar) for setup.
Open for the team to decide — flagged as needing planning.

---

## 5. How it relates to what's built

- Per-activity **team stage** (discussion/collective/individual) stays as-is — that's *inside one
  activity*. The Team Tab is the *cross-activity* project space that references activities but outlives them.
- The AI **Project Progress Report** reuses the AI-proposes/faculty-approves pattern (§11): it's a
  draft the faculty can review, never auto-authoritative.
- Peer evaluation is the existing ◇ deferred item — the Team Tab is its natural home.

---

## 6. Open questions for the team

1. Is the Team Tab **course-wide** (one persistent team per student for the term) or **per-project**
   (teams re-formed per activity)? The whiteboard reads course-wide/project-persistent.
2. Audio discussion — record in-browser vs upload a file? (Recording needs mic capture, which the
   module scope excludes — likely **upload** for the pilot.)
3. Progress Report cadence — regenerate on every resource add, or on demand?
4. Faculty placement — Analytics vs Course content (§4).
5. CATME/peer-eval — in for the pilot, or keep ◇ deferred?
