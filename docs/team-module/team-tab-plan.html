Team Tab — Revamp Plan

Make the tab where faculty run team activities feel calm and obvious.
Draft for reviewScope: the Team tab onlyNothing else in the app changes
In scope: the Team tab — its Activities / Teams / Analytics sub-tabs and the activity workspace you open from it. Out of scope (untouched): Course Designer (Create), Library, the student experience, and top-level navigation.
01

The Team tab today

Sub-tab 1
Activities
A list of team activities with a status badge and an Open button. Where you land.
Sub-tab 2
Teams
Class teams — create, add/remove students, roles, target size, per-activity customization.
Sub-tab 3
Analytics
Prep/on-time stats + the prep-response & transcription review.

Opening an activity leads to its workspace (Source · Resources · Discussion · Progress, with a Faculty / Student preview).

02

What feels bad

  1. The Teams sub-tab buries the teams.
    Four control rows — Target size, New team, the Same / Per-activity toggle, the “Teams for” dropdown — stack up before a single team is visible. The config shouts louder than the content.
  2. Forming teams is a chore.
    “Add student” adds one person at a time from an invisible pool. There’s no way to seat a whole roster at once.
  3. Nothing says “what do I do next.”
    Activities / Teams / Analytics read as three equal peers. Landing on the tab, there’s no signal of what’s urgent — whose prep needs review, what’s due, which teams have submitted.
  4. Prep & transcription review is hidden.
    The daily job of checking student prep sits three clicks deep — behind a stat tile inside Analytics — instead of one obvious click.
  5. Every row looks equally important.
    The activity list is a wall of identical “Open” buttons with no primary; the workspace is a flat set of tabs. Nothing guides the eye.
03

Principles

Content first, config tucked away.
Teams and activities appear immediately; setup (size, per-activity, scope) lives behind a small control, not stacked on top.
One primary action per screen.
Exactly one filled navy button for the main verb (New team / Open); everything else is quiet.
The tab orients you.
A one-line “needs attention” strip up top answers “what’s urgent” in a glance.
Keep the power, don’t lead with it.
Per-activity teams and roles stay — they’re just not the first thing you trip over.
04

Redesign · Teams sub-tab

The headline fix. Same capabilities, calmer surface.

Before
  • Row 1: Target size + New team
  • Row 2: Same / Per-activity toggle
  • Row 3: “Teams for” dropdown
  • …then, finally, the team cards
After
  • One header line: Class teams + Form teams (primary) · New team · a Team setup ⚙ button
  • Team cards immediately below
  • The ⚙ popover holds target size, the per-activity toggle & scope
  • Per-activity on → a slim “Teams for: [activity]” pill above the cards
Form teams new
Auto-distributes the whole roster into teams (Random or Balanced), respecting the target size — instead of seating students one at a time.
Add student → searchable picker
Opens a searchable list of unassigned students to place, rather than blindly pulling the next one off a hidden pool.
Roles inline
Each member has a small role chip you click to set (Recorder, Presenter…) — no separate mode.
Per-activity teams: kept, tucked
Lives in the Team-setup popover; when on, the scope pill makes it obvious which activity you’re editing. Your customization lever stays.
05

Redesign · Activities sub-tab

A “needs attention” strip on top
One quiet line: “3 prep responses to review · Bridge Design due Wed · 4 of 6 teams submitted.” The tab now tells you where to look.
Cleaner rows, one action
Each row: activity name, team-based · mode · pts, a status badge, due date, and a single consistent Open. No more wall of equal buttons.
Status means something
Draft / Scheduled / Team stage / Grading stay, but read as a real state, sorted so the live ones sit up top.
06

Redesign · Analytics & workspace

Surface prep review
The Prep responses / Needs review screen becomes a clear card & button in Analytics (and reachable from the activity), not a buried stat tile. Same review flow, one obvious click.
Trim the vanity stats
Keep prep-complete, on-time, and the flagged/awaiting counts (they drive the attention strip); drop anything that’s decoration.
Workspace: light touch
Keep Source · Resources · Discussion · Progress and the Faculty / Student preview. Just give each its one primary action and consistent buttons — no restructure.
07

Build order

  1. Declutter the Teams sub-tab — teams-first layout, one primary, the Team-setup ⚙ popover. (The win you flagged.)
  2. Form teams + searchable Add student — make seating a roster fast.
  3. Attention strip + cleaner rows on Activities.
  4. Surface prep/transcription review out of the buried stat tile.
08

Explicitly not changing

Course Designer (Create), Library, the student experience, and the top-level tabs are untouched. Per-activity teams and roles stay. This is a tidy-up of one tab, not a rethink.
